# ESBMC

**Kind:** system

The Efficient SMT-based Context-Bounded Model Checker for C. It labels the FormAI-v2 vulnerabilities with counterexamples, which keeps false positives low ([[sun-2024-formai-v2]]), and it is the checker in the [[esbmc-ai-2023]] repair loop. Cordeiro's Manchester group is behind both.
